FOUNDATIONAL IDENTITY & ETHOS

Mission and Methodology

Shiksha Sopan is a non-governmental organization (NGO) whose purpose is to impart value- based education to economically weaker section of the society. Its various activities are spreaded in and around the lush-green campus of IIT Kanpur. Shiksha Sopan's philosophy stands on three pillars of Shiksha, Sanskar, and Swavalamban. We believe that education is not merely loading children with information. Therefore, we try our best to establish a connection of class-room teaching with daily-life activities and behaviors. With this point in mind, we keep organizing various activities that are aimed to further nurture these values.

I
शिक्षा
Shiksha • Education

Connecting textbook physics and classroom concepts to daily-life activities and practical observations.

II
संस्कार
Sanskar • Values & Ethics

Instilling empathy, ethical consciousness, mutual respect, and social gratitude in every child.

III
स्वावलंबन
Swavalamban • Self-Reliance

Nurturing practical problem-solving skills so students become self-reliant and productive citizens.

NATIONAL ANVESHIKA COMPETITION

Competition on Experimental Skills — NAEST 2025

The National Anveshika Experimental Skill Test (NAEST) is India's most prestigious annual hands-on physics assessment. Conceived by Prof. H.C. Verma and organized by the Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT), NAEST evaluates school and college students on genuine observational intuition, analytical reasoning, and causal deduction rather than textbook memorization.
