OUR THREE REGULAR FUNCTIONS

Marking Important Milestones on the Annual Calendar

12 JAN
Annual Function
Vivekananda Jayanti Week

Swami Vivekananda Birth Anniversary & Annual Cultural Week

Celebrated during the week containing January 12th, this is Shiksha Sopan's flagship celebration. Programs are open to the entire village community around the IITK campus, fostering vibrant participation and cultural pride.

  • Week-Long Competitions: Rangoli-making, debates, essay competitions, general & science quizzes, drawing/painting, and singing patriotic and nationalistic songs.
  • Social Awareness Rally: 500 to 600 children and adults march through Barasirohi village carrying placards on girl-child education, clean environment, water conservation, and anti-tobacco awareness.
  • Grand Cultural Stage Show: A 2.5-hour cultural extravaganza staged in Barasirohi where children showcase their talents in dramatics, folk dances, musical skits, and public oration.
15 AUG
Independence Day
Lecture Hall L-7, IITK

Independence Day Celebrations at IIT Kanpur

Held inside the premier Lecture Hall L-7 at IIT Kanpur, this function is designed to foster a sense of national commitment and remember the sacrifices of freedom fighters.

  • Children from all Shiksha Sopan learning centers gather on the IITK campus.
  • Presentation of short theatrical skits related to the Indian freedom struggle and pivotal social reforms.
  • Patriotic songs and speeches by children and distinguished IITK faculty members.
02 OCT
Gandhi Jayanti
Barasirohi Vidyalaya

Mahatma Gandhi & Lal Bahadur Shastri Jayanti

A solemn day dedicated to reaffirming commitments to a corruption-free society based on truth, non-violence, simplicity, and national well-being.

  • Children at Barasirohi Vidyalaya stage skits illustrating key episodes from the lives of Mahatma Gandhi and Lal Bahadur Shastri.
  • Discussions on swachhata (cleanliness), ethical conduct, and social service.
  • Community pledges toward non-violence, mutual harmony, and truthfulness.
