INSTITUTIONAL STEWARDSHIP

Steering with Purpose, Educating with Heart.

IIT Kanpur Faculty & Staff Registered NGO Governance Shiksha • Sanskar • Swavalamban

Shiksha Sopan is governed by an Executive Committee composed of esteemed professors, technical officers, scholars, and dedicated grassroots volunteers from the IIT Kanpur fraternity and local community.

The committee provides strategic leadership and ethical guidance, overseeing key initiatives including the National Anveshika Experimental Skill Test (NAEST), Dr. V.N. Kulkarni Scholarships, Sopan Ashram Residential Short Courses, and daily rural learning centers.
